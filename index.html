How to get an internship or job at Google

A general guide for students. Programs, eligibility and dates change every year, so always confirm on the official pages.

1. Build strong fundamentals
  • Learn data structures and algorithms: arrays, strings, hashing, trees, graphs and dynamic programming.
  • Practise daily on LeetCode, Codeforces or CodeChef, and explain your solutions out loud.
  • Pick one language, such as C++, Java or Python, and know it well.
2. Programs for students
  • Google Summer of Code: a paid, remote open-source programme. Start contributing to an organisation before applications open.
  • Google Careers: search for student and early-career roles and internships, and check each role's eligibility.
  • Google Cloud Skills Boost: hands-on labs and learning paths.
  • Google Developer Groups: communities, events and challenges.
3. Build proof of work
  • Ship two or three real projects and put them on GitHub with clear READMEs.
  • Contribute to open source, even small fixes and documentation.
  • Keep a one-page resume with impact: what you built, for whom, and the result.
4. The interview process
  • Application, then a recruiter conversation.
  • Technical interviews, usually online, where you solve coding problems while explaining your thinking.
  • Further interview rounds, then a review and team matching before an offer.
  • Prepare by doing mock interviews with club seniors.
5. Tips that help
  • Apply early and apply to several roles.
  • Build a network: talk to seniors and alumni, and ask for referrals only after you have prepared.
  • Communicate clearly: Google interviewers care about how you think, not only the final answer.
